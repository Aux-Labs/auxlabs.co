# Image slots — who makes what

Every open slot on the site, its exact filename and size, and who owns it.
A file dropped at the path appears on the site; until then the designed plate shows.
**One owner per slot.** Samantha's files win: an existing file is never
overwritten without Imran's say-so. Claude Design works only on slots marked
`CLAUDE DESIGN` and only ever delivers JPGs. It does not touch site code.

House style (from Samantha's finished plates: commercial, cultural, courts, merge,
Michelin): a double exposure of a real photographic subject and a second image
(blueprint, chart, street, texture), toned to a single deep green
(~#0B6B2E) on pale mint (~#E9F3EC), high contrast, no added type, logos or watermarks.
