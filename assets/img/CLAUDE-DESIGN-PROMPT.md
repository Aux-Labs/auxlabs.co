## The house style

- **Double exposure.** Every plate fuses two images: a real photographic subject, and a second image that comments on it. That second image can be a blueprint, a chart, a street, a texture or a found document. In "commercial" a stack of sculpted heads carries a Federal Reserve money-flow diagram. In "Michelin", tire treads run under a plated dish.
- **One colour.** Toned to a single deep green (about #0B6B2E) on pale mint (about #E9F3EC). Lights blow out toward mint, and shadows go to solid green. No other hues.
- **Photographic, not illustrated.** These read as treated photographs and archival documents. No vector art, no cartoon, no 3D render look.
- **No added type.** No headlines, logos, captions or watermarks. Text is allowed only when it's part of a found document inside the image, like the "Federal Reserve" label in her blueprint.
- **Restraint.** One strong subject per plate, generous pale space, nothing busy. Samantha and I agreed not to widen the palette.

## Rules

1. **Sources.** Use only images you have the right to use: public-domain or CC0 photographs (for example Wikimedia Commons public-domain files, the Library of Congress, or NASA), or images you generate yourself. No stock photos with licence terms, no recognizable private individuals, and no brand logos. For every plate, list the source images you used, with links and licences.
2. **Deliver JPGs only.** sRGB, under 500 KB each, at the exact filenames and sizes above. Don't build a web page, and don't write or change any site code. The site already has a slot waiting for each file.
3. **Stay in your lane.** Samantha is making `axl-wp-01.jpg` and `axl-wp-03.jpg`. Don't make those two, and don't re-make anything already on the site.
