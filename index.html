§ 01.00 // THE DIAGNOSIS

Welcome to the Pretend Era™.

Most people privately stopped believing the official story years ago: about the media, the market, the mission statement, the meeting they just sat through. But each of them assumes everyone else still believes, so everyone keeps performing. Social scientists call this pluralistic ignorance: a norm nobody holds, enforced by everybody.

Trust collapsed because honesty became individually irrational. The first person to say "this isn't working" pays the price, so nobody goes first. And you cannot message your way out of a pretend equilibrium. No campaign makes honesty rational. Mechanisms do: rules, defaults, sequences, and incentives that make the honest move the easy move.

That's what we build. We call it behavioral infrastructure.

§ 02.00 // THE ARBITRAGE

The persuasion industry is aimed at the wrong problems.

In 2020, junior strategists at brand agencies had a better working model of how information moves through a networked population than the institutions responsible for public trust. They had spent a decade inside the live information environment. The institutions were still writing press releases for a world that had ended.

Some of the best behavioral scientists of our generation work for sneaker brands. Many more work at Facebook and Google, tuning the feeds our research program studies as machinery of the collapse. The craft is real. The assignments have been the problem.

Aux Labs runs that arbitrage on purpose: the same craft, pointed at the problems underneath the problems.

We do not sell attention. Attention is not behavior.

§ 03.00 // THE METHOD

The field comes first.

Five stages, one team. Qual, quant, and desk research, in whatever mix the question demands; the mix starts where the behavior is.

Method_01

Go to the source.

We don't resell other people's summaries. The work starts with primary material: the street, the archive, the transcript, the dataset. We go where the behavior is and stay long enough to hear the thing nobody types into a form. The strongest case in our record started exactly that way.

Method_02

Find It.

The hidden cost, or the untapped trust, sitting inside a system you already pay for: a schedule, a route, a policy, a queue, a public.

Method_03

Prove It.

Quantify it, so the mechanism has a business case and a baseline.

Method_04

Build It.

Medium-agnostic: research, product, film, print, software. Whatever form the mechanism needs to exist in the world.

Method_05

Measure It.

Legitimacy compounds. We instrument it so you can watch it compound.

We do all five in one team, because the handoffs between them are where this work usually dies. If your question can be answered from a desk, we'll say so in the first call and point you at someone cheaper.

The method has an academic name: applied mechanism design, the Nobel-line economics of Hurwicz, Maskin, and Myerson. What it buys you is simpler to say: systems where the better behavior is the easier behavior.
