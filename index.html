§ 01.00 // THE DIAGNOSIS

Welcome to the Pretend Era™.

Most people privately stopped believing the official story years ago: about the media, the market, the mission statement, the meeting they just sat through. But each of them assumes everyone else still believes, so everyone keeps performing. Social scientists call this pluralistic ignorance: a norm nobody holds, enforced by everybody.

That is the actual crisis underneath the crises. Trust didn't collapse because people got cynical. It collapsed because honesty became individually irrational. The first person to say "this isn't working" pays the price, so nobody goes first.

And the diagnosis tells you something no strategy deck will: you cannot message your way out of a pretend equilibrium. No campaign makes honesty rational. Only mechanisms do: rules, defaults, sequences, and incentives that make the honest move the easy move, and the trustworthy institution the cheap one to believe.

That's what we build. We call it behavioral infrastructure.

§ 02.00 // THE ARBITRAGE

The persuasion industry is aimed at the wrong problems.

In 2020, junior strategists at brand agencies carried a more accurate working model of how information actually moves through a networked population than the public institutions whose job that was. They had spent the previous decade inside the live information environment; the institutions were still writing press releases for a world that had ended.

The craft is real, and it is concentrated in strange places. Some of the best behavioral scientists of our generation work for sneaker brands. Many more work at Facebook and Google, tuning the feeds our research program studies as machinery of the collapse. The craft was never the villain. The assignment was.

Aux Labs runs the arbitrage deliberately: fifteen years of that craft, pointed at the problems underneath the problems. Same training. Different assignment.

We do not sell attention. Attention is not behaviour.

§ 03.00 // THE METHOD

The field comes first.

Five stages, one team. Qual, quant, and desk research, in whatever mix the question demands; the mix starts where the behavior is.

Method_01

Go to the source.

We don't resell other people's summaries. The work starts with primary material: the street, the archive, the transcript, the dataset. We go where the behavior is and stay long enough to hear the thing nobody types into a form. The strongest case in our record started exactly that way.

Method_02

Find It.

The hidden cost, or the untapped trust, sitting inside a system you already pay for: a schedule, a route, a policy, a queue, a public.

Method_03

Prove It.

Quantify it, so the mechanism has a business case and a baseline.

Method_04

Build It.

Medium-agnostic: research, product, film, print, software. Whatever form the mechanism needs to exist in the world.

Method_05

Measure It.

Legitimacy compounds. We instrument it so you can watch it compound.

We do all five in one team, because the handoffs between them are where this work usually dies.

And if your question can be answered from a desk, we'll say so in the first call and point you at someone cheaper. Going is not a premium we charge for. It's the condition of taking the work.

The method has an academic name: applied mechanism design, from the Nobel-line economics of Hurwicz, Maskin, and Myerson. You don't need the name. You need the result: systems where the better behavior is the easier behavior.
