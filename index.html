§ 02.00 // THE FIELD

Behavioral infrastructure.

Roads and power grids are infrastructure everyone can see. Trust, legitimacy and the sense that honesty pays are infrastructure too, and almost nobody maintains them. That is the field we work in: we measure where that layer is failing, then build the rules, defaults and incentives that repair it.

§ 03.00 // THE DIAGNOSIS

Most people privately stopped believing the official story years ago. Each assumes everyone else still believes, so everyone keeps performing.

You cannot message your way out of that. Mechanisms can: rules, defaults, and incentives that make the honest move the easy move. We build them. The full argument is in the research.
