TRM_01 [ HOW_WE_MAKE_DEALS ]

Enthusiastic consent,
or no deal.

Every agreement here has to pass one test: both sides walk away happy about the number. Not resigned. Not "fine." Happy.

Most consulting deals are a game of poker. You hide your budget, we hide our floor, and whoever flinches first loses a little respect for the other. It's a bad way to start a relationship that's supposed to fix trust.

So we play a different game. We work out, together, what Aux Labs should offer for the budget and opportunity you're actually glad to put in. Then we leave room for the mess: the false starts, the findings nobody expected, the part where the plan meets the building. That part isn't a cost of the work. It's the part we like best.

[ THE_TEST ]

Would you be happy paying it?

Would we be happy doing it?

Two yeses or nothing. A no costs nobody anything.

Consent Checklist

  • Both numbers said out loud
  • Nobody is talked into anything
  • Either side can stop, at any step
TRM_02 // THE_HAPPY_NUMBER

Four moves.
No poker.

01

You name your happy number.

Not your ceiling. The amount you'd feel good about spending on this, a year from now, even if the finding is inconvenient.

02

We name ours.

What we'd be glad to do the work for, and what else would make it worth doing: the data, the case study, the door it opens.

03

If they don't meet, we change the shape.

Smaller scope. A first phase. A different mix of cash and evidence. We resize the offer until both numbers feel good. We never lean on anyone's feelings to close the gap.

04

If nothing fits, we part friends.

And we'll usually tell you who might be a better fit. A clean no is a good outcome. A grudging yes is the only bad one.

ADAPTED FROM JOE HUDSON'S HAPPY MONEY GAME (ART OF ACCOMPLISHMENT). THE ADAPTATION, AND ANY MISTAKES IN IT, ARE OURS.

TRM_04 // WORKING_MODELS

Pick a shape.
Or invent one with us.

MODEL_01

Day rate.

The simplest deal. You buy days, we spend them on your problem. Good for a diagnosis, a workshop, or a second opinion.

MODEL_02

Scoped sprint.

A fixed fee for a fixed question, with a finish line both sides can see. Good when the budget is set and the question is sharp.

MODEL_03

Ghostwritten CMO.

A monthly retainer. We work inside your team, in your voice, under your name, from strategy down to the sentence. More below.

MODEL_04

Evidence trade.

Less cash, in exchange for data access and the right to publish what we learn. Your problem becomes a case study for the field.

MODEL_05

Grant partner.

For nonprofits and public bodies. We write the proposal with you, name the research role in it, and get paid if the funding comes through.

MODEL_06

Outcome share.

Part of the fee rides on a result we agree to measure before we start. If the mechanism works, we both win. If not, you paid less.

TRM_05 [ WHAT_YOU'RE_HIRING ]

A CMO who goes
deeper than a CMO.

A good CMO moves the brand. We're a think-and-do tank, not only a research firm, so we also go after the thing underneath the brand: whether people have a reason to trust it, and whether the good it claims to do actually happens.

Your voice goes out under your name. The evidence goes out under ours, with your consent, under the Firewall's rules.

One principal. One unified theory. Every discipline it took to build it, pointed at your problem.
